Diagrams

Two kinds of picture over one document. A sequence diagram lays out the actors and applications in a flow, wires them with hard and soft links, numbers the steps, branches them on a case, and reads as a free canvas or as a UML sequence diagram. A deployment diagram lays out the nodes of an application inside groups — regions, datacenters, tiers, nested as deep as they really are — each with the LTM or VIP fronting it. Both are JSON you can edit, and both are searchable by server name.
